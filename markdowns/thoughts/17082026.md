A few people might think I am a Global Citizen, and I did think about that too and once described
myself as such. But taking a look at the definition on Google I quickly realise how stupid the
definition is. Like I don't think I care too much about the "values" and "ethics" bloat about seeing
global humanity as a single family or something like that (it just feels like "moral kidnapping" by
political activists to me), but more about global accessibility and lifting of localised
restrictions in favor of globalised talent and meritocracy.

Personally, I enjoy understanding and exploring a variety of cultures (through language immersion),
and this has also given me the ability to be entertained by a wider set of media and gain knowledge
through communicating with different people across the world. But to associate my "Global
Citizenship" with obstructive climate obligations and virtue signaling seems bit of an overstrech..?
