Nowadays it's really easy to see if a written piece of text is written by a human or AI generated.
To circumvent this, one strategy that I developed naturally is to write in British English while
occasionally adopting American spellings in a few. This combination almost never happens with AI.

For example: "He specialises in color therapy." Here we use the British "Specialise" instead of
the American "Specialize", and the American "Color" instead of the British "Colour".

It's not really consistent, but i'm sure any real person who isn't a clanker would appreciate
the subtle cues revealing that someone actually paid the effort and time to write their own texts.
