First Class Honours in degree classification, is that I will now only grind my
elective (technical) papers for raw score. There's not really any point grinding
in the B2E1/E2 "Entrepreneurship and Management" papers per say because even if
you do so the max score you'll get is 75 since these are graded qualitatively.

returns, especially with the 0.5 credit deficit putting me at a disadvantage
compared to other non-EEM peers when talking about pure rank.

My rationale (definitely contains some cope, but view it with logic too) is that
I am not really "competing" with others because we are fundamentally on
different tracks. Rather "score" itself is the most realistic metric/indicator
of your raw abilities and therefore the ability to minmax the ones you actually
give a shit about is more rewarding than beating 99% of nerds in every domain,
pretty much what all 4 years of the MEng degree requires as well.
