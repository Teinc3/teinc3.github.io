*Last Updated: 04/11/2024*
*Genre: Politics*

US Presidential Election. Donald Trump is now trying on his 3rd attempt to win
the election, and standing up against him is Kamala Harris.

I have vastly different perspectives on the election,
and I hope Kamala Harris wins the election.

government bureaucracy and censorship is not the way to go, and that the US
