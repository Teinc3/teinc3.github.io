## What The Quant
*Last updated: 06/09/2026*
*Genre: Society*

Sometimes I ask myself why do all the cracked nerds around me seem to go into trading.
Is it because of the money? Probably. But why let money dictate your life? It's a tool not a goal.

I see one problem with Trading and that it is a very involutionary sector/industry.
Essentially, any skills that you gain from trading is hyperspecific and non-transferrable.
The closest adjacent exit out of the Trading ecosystem would probably be AI/ML labs.
And that I respect 100% more, simply because it actually gives some sort of real world impact,
with improvements to the base models themselves actually improving the overall infrastructure.

If you have big goals and aim to use liquidity as capital for pursuing them (for example,
starting your own tech venture), then yes you can 100% free yourself from VC negotiation,
but you still can't bypass the management layer when you ultimately have to scale things.
And Quantitative finance itself is completely different from traditional investment banking,
which runs closer to what a VC firm actually does. My instinct (correct me if I'm wrong) is that
training in macronomic information processing and decision making is more critical in VCs.

The only reason particular goals and just wish to retire early, but then it feels wasted.
You spent years grinding from HS to Uni to a Trading firm, having earned such a capital leverage,
then you decide to just let it rot away overtime. But that's probably just a personality thing.
Or maybe the quant mentality is extremely risk-averse and completely ignores sunken cost,
so any grinding in the prior years are simply discarded as non-factors. I guess that's the case!
