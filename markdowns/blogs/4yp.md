# Options for 4YP
*Last updated: 16/10/2026*
*Genre: Life Planning*

For Oxford Engineers the 4th year contains 6 credits, 3 of which are allocated
to a final year project and the other 3 to 6 technical modules (each 0.5 creds).

With my main plan of obtaining a placement then using it to convert to an offer
foiled (read my earlier blog post earlier) the probability of an external
24-week placement dwindles significantly. However, I don't really want to do a
simple 3 month SWE internship with no amazing value then go back to a 4YP for
the year. So, here's the plan:

A future spinout from OUI is highly likely considering the work I'm already
doing in TrustedMDT. So, why don't I just let the spinout hire me for a 6 month
placement where I focus on scaling the suite to handle the traffic for US client
hospital sites and taking iterative feedback from them? The amount of
"entrepreneurship" from this project easily ticks whatever boxes the department
requires so I don't get marks for the 3 credits completely fucked. The spinout
is 100% from Oxford so the grading panel will have a "closeness bias" to it much
like any other theoretical 4YP, unlike the "alienness" of external placements.
And while medical trials for the product are still being developed, I can focus
my entire time on grinding my ass on 3rd year papers and preparing for next
year's requisition already having the CV value locked.

And even if I can't get the placement, I can still continue "research work" on
it as a topic for 4YP. So the stuff that I already do, I can continue doing. I
don't have to bother finding a separate project at all.

Sounds like an awesome combination of factors, honestly. Let's see how it goes.
